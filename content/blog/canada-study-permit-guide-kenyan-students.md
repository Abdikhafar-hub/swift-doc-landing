## Overview of the Canadian Study Permit

A Canadian Study Permit allows international students from Kenya to study at Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs) across Canada. To qualify, students must demonstrate academic eligibility, sufficient financial resources to cover tuition and living expenses, and a clear plan to depart Canada upon completion of studies.

## Core Document Checklist for Kenyan Students

### 1. Academic & Institutional Documentation
- **Letter of Acceptance (LOA):** Official unconditional offer letter from a Canadian Designated Learning Institution (DLI) containing the DLI number.
- **Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL):** Mandatory attestation letter issued by the respective province/territory where the institution is located.
- **Academic Transcripts & Certificates:** Certified copies of KCSE certificates, undergraduate degree certificates, and university transcripts.

### 2. Proof of Financial Support
Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) requires proof that you can pay for your first year of tuition plus living expenses (minimum CAD 20,635 for single students outside Quebec).
- **Tuition Fee Receipts:** Official receipt demonstrating payment of first-term or first-year tuition.
- **Bank Statements:** Certified 4 to 6 months bank statements of the student or designated sponsor (parents/legal guardians).
- **Sponsorship Affidavit:** If funded by parents or family, an affidavit of financial support accompanied by the sponsor's employment letters, business registration (CR12/PIN), and payslips.
- **Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC):** If applying through specialized fast-track streams where applicable.

### 3. Statement of Purpose (Study Plan)
A well-structured Statement of Purpose is critical. It must address:
- Why you chose this specific program and institution in Canada.
- How the qualification aligns with your previous academic background and future career in Kenya.
- Ties to Kenya (family, career opportunities, property) establishing your intention to return.

## Medical Exam and Police Clearance

- **Upfront Medical Examination:** Conducted by an IRCC-approved panel physician in Nairobi (such as IOM Nairobi).
- **Certificate of Good Conduct:** Issued by Kenya Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) / eCitizen.

## Biometrics & Submission in Nairobi

1. Submit the study permit application online via the official IRCC secure account portal.
2. Pay the IRCC processing and biometric fees.
3. Receive the Biometric Instruction Letter (BIL) and schedule an appointment at the VFS Global Canada Visa Application Centre in Nairobi.
