## Overview of Kenyan Work and Entry Permits

Under the Kenya Citizenship and Immigration Act, foreign nationals who wish to engage in employment, business, trade, or voluntary work in Kenya must obtain an authorized Entry Permit (Work Permit) issued by the Directorate of Immigration Services (Nyayo House, Nairobi).

Engaging in any employment or commercial activity in Kenya on a visitor eTA or tourist visa is strictly prohibited by law.

## Major Work Permit Classes in Kenya

### 1. Class D: Employment Permit
Issued to individuals who have been offered employment in Kenya by a registered company or organisation.
- **Key Requirement:** The employer must demonstrate that the expatriate possesses specialized skills, qualifications, or technical knowledge that are not readily available in the Kenyan domestic labour market.
- **Understudy Requirement:** The employer must designate a qualified Kenyan citizen understudy who will undergo training to eventually take over the position.
- **Documentation:** Curriculum Vitae (CV), certified academic and professional degree certificates, signed employment contract, Kenyan understudy's CV and National ID, company Certificate of Incorporation, CR12, and KRA Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC).

### 2. Class G: Investors & Business Owners Permit
Issued to foreign nationals who intend to engage in a specific trade, business, consultancy, or manufacture in Kenya.
- **Key Requirement:** Proof of substantial investment capital (statutory minimum of USD 100,000 or equivalent) held in a Kenyan commercial bank account or proven capital equipment investment.
- **Documentation:** Proof of investment capital (bank audit statements, equipment import documents), Certificate of Incorporation, CR12 search, single business permit, and company KRA PIN.

### 3. Class I: Religious, Charitable & NGO Permit
Issued to members of accredited missionary societies, religious orders, or registered Non-Governmental Organisations (NGOs) in Kenya.
- **Documentation:** NGO Registration Board certificate, recommendation letter from the NGO Coordination Board, academic/theological qualifications, and police clearance from the country of origin.

## Special Pass: Temporary Short-Term Work Authorisation

When an expatriate is required in Kenya for urgent, short-term assignments (such as machinery installation, technical audits, emergency engineering, or while a formal Class D permit application is pending review), employers can apply for a **Special Pass**.
- Valid for up to **90 days**, renewable once for a total maximum of **6 months**.
- Allows the foreign specialist to lawfully work immediately while the substantive long-term permit undergoes committee review.

## Post-Approval: Foreigner Certificate (Alien Card)

Once a work permit is approved and the statutory government issuance fees are settled, all foreign residents residing in Kenya for more than 90 days must register for a **Foreigner Certificate (Alien Card)** at the Directorate of Immigration Services (Nyayo House).
