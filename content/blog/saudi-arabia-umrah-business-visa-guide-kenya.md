## Overview of Saudi Arabia Visas from Kenya

The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA) offers several visa categories for Kenyan citizens, including Umrah Pilgrimage Visas, Commercial/Business Visit Visas, Family Visit Visas, and Work Visas.

Consular visa processing and biometric enrolment in Nairobi are outsourced by the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) to **Tasheer (VFS Tasheer Visa Services Centre)** located in Nairobi.

## Common Saudi Visa Categories for Kenyans

### 1. Umrah Pilgrimage Visa
Designed for Kenyan Muslims undertaking the Umrah pilgrimage to the holy cities of Makkah and Madinah outside the official Hajj season:
- **Valid Passport:** Minimum 6 months validity.
- **Vaccination Records:** Meningococcal ACWY vaccination certificate and compliance with prevailing Saudi health directives.
- **Confirmed Travel:** Round-trip flight tickets and confirmed hotel booking in Makkah / Madinah through certified pilgrimage operators or platforms (such as Nusuk).
- **Women & Minors Guidelines:** Compliant with Saudi regulatory rules regarding family travel.

### 2. Commercial / Business Visit Visa
For corporate executives, investors, and technical representatives visiting Saudi partners or attending exhibitions:
- **MOFA Electronic Visa Invitation:** Issued by the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs via the inviting Saudi company's chamber of commerce registration.
- **Kenyan Company Letter:** Stating the candidate's designation, purpose of travel, duration, and financial guarantee.
- **Commercial Registration (CR):** Copy of the inviting Saudi company's Commercial Registration.
- **Kenyan Business Documents:** Certificate of Incorporation and KRA PIN/TCC.

### 3. Work / Employment Visa
For professionals recruited by Saudi employers:
- Requires verified employment contract approved by the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD), legalized educational certificates (authenticated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Kenya and the Saudi Embassy), and approved medical clearance from a GAMCA-accredited medical centre in Nairobi.

## Tasheer Centre Submission Process in Nairobi

1. **MOFA Electronic Registration:** Register application data on the official Saudi Enjaz / MOFA portal.
2. **Book Tasheer Appointment:** Schedule biometric enrolment on the official Tasheer portal.
3. **Biometrics & Submission:** Attend Tasheer centre in Nairobi for document review, fee settlement, and biometric capture (fingerprints and iris scan).
4. **Visa Stamping:** Typical turnaround is **3 to 7 working days**.
