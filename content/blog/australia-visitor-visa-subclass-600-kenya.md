## Overview of the Australia Visitor Visa (Subclass 600)

The Visitor Visa (Subclass 600) allows Kenyan passport holders to travel to Australia for tourism, visiting family and friends, or short-term business visitor activities (such as attending trade fairs or contract discussions). It is typically granted for stays of 3, 6, or 12 months.

Australian visa applications are processed 100% digitally through the Department of Home Affairs **ImmiAccount** portal, followed by biometric data collection at the Australian Visa Application Centre (VFS Global) in Nairobi.

## The Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) Assessment

The Australian Department of Home Affairs evaluates all Subclass 600 applications strictly against the **Genuine Temporary Entrant** criteria. Case officers assess whether the applicant genuinely intends to stay temporarily in Australia and return to Kenya, taking into account:
- Economic and employment circumstances in Kenya.
- Ties to Kenya (family, assets, business, ongoing obligations).
- Travel history to other international destinations.
- The applicant’s immigration compliance history.

## Core Document Checklist for Kenyan Applicants

### 1. Identity & Travel Documents
- **Valid Passport:** Bio-data page copy valid for the duration of intended stay.
- **National Identity Card:** Clear scan of Kenyan National ID.
- **Previous Travel History:** Clear colour scans of entry/exit stamps and visas from previous travels (e.g., UK, USA, Schengen, UAE).

### 2. Proof of Financial Capacity
- **Certified Bank Statements:** 6 months personal and/or company bank statements showing regular operational income.
- **Source of Wealth:** Tax returns (KRA PIN and Tax Compliance Certificate), land title deeds, commercial leases, or investment certificates.

### 3. Proof of Employment & Ties to Kenya
- **Employed:** Formal employer letter confirming job title, salary, tenure, approved leave dates, and confirmation of position held on return; accompanied by recent 3 to 6 months payslips.
- **Business Owners:** Certificate of Incorporation, recent CR12 search, single business permit, and business tax filings.
- **Family Ties:** Marriage certificate, children's birth certificates.

### 4. Purpose of Stay
- **Tourism:** Planned travel itinerary, accommodation bookings, planned sightseeing.
- **Family Visit:** Statutory declaration / invitation letter from the host in Australia, proof of host's Australian citizenship or permanent residency, and evidence of host's accommodation.
- **Business Visitor:** Formal invitation letter from the Australian host company and a supporting letter from your Kenyan employer.

## Biometrics Enrolment at VFS Nairobi

Once your application is submitted on ImmiAccount, the Department of Home Affairs will generate a **Requirement to Provide Biometrics** letter. You must schedule an appointment and attend the Australian Biometric Collection Centre at VFS Global in Nairobi within 14 days of receiving the notice.
