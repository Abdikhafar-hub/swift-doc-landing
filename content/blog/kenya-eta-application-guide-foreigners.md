## What is the Kenya Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA)?

The Government of Kenya, through the State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services, implemented the **Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA)** system, replacing traditional visas for all foreign visitors entering the Republic of Kenya.

All foreign nationals (except citizens of East African Community partner states) must possess an approved Kenya eTA before boarding their flight to Kenya.

## Who Needs a Kenya eTA?

- **Tourists & Vacationers:** Visiting Kenya for safari, beach holidays, or cultural exploration.
- **Business Visitors:** Attending corporate conferences, meetings, or trade exhibitions.
- **Visiting Family & Friends:** Traveling to visit Kenyan citizens or resident expatriates.
- **Transit Travelers:** Leaving the airport during international connections.

## Required Documents for Kenya eTA Application

To process an eTA application smoothly, travelers must prepare:
1. **Valid Passport:** Valid for at least 6 months from the date of planned arrival in Kenya, with at least one blank page.
2. **Passport Photograph / Selfie:** Clear digital portrait photo taken against a plain background.
3. **Contact Details:** Email address and active telephone number.
4. **Travel Information:** Confirmed flight arrival and departure details (airline, flight number, date).
5. **Accommodation Proof:** Confirmed hotel reservation or, if staying with a host, a signed Invitation Letter and a copy of the host's Kenyan National ID or Alien Card/Work Permit.
6. **Yellow Fever Vaccination Certificate:** Required if arriving from or transiting through yellow fever endemic countries.

## East Africa Tourist Visa (EATV)

For travelers visiting **Kenya, Uganda, and Rwanda** on a single journey, the **East Africa Tourist Visa (EATV)** provides 90 days of multiple-entry access across all three nations. The application must be submitted to the immigration authority of the country that serves as your first point of entry into East Africa.

## Turnaround Time and Submission Deadlines

- Standard processing typically takes **24 to 72 hours**.
- However, applicants are advised to lodge their eTA at least **3 days before departure** to accommodate potential identity verifications.
