## What is VFS Global Nairobi?

VFS Global is the official outsourced commercial partner contracted by numerous foreign governments and diplomatic missions in Nairobi—including the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, South Africa, Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, Austria, and Japan—to manage administrative visa intake and biometric data collection.

VFS Global staff **do not make visa decisions** or evaluate the merits of your application. Their role is strictly administrative: verifying document presence, collecting fingerprints and photographs, and transmitting physical passports and digital files to consular decision-makers.

## Where is VFS Global Located in Nairobi?

VFS Global operates major centres in Nairobi, commonly situated in:
- **Parklands / Westlands / Highridge:** Major hubs for the UK, Canada, Australia, Schengen missions, and South Africa.

*Always verify the exact floor and building specified on your official Appointment Confirmation Letter before setting off.*

## Essential Rules for Your VFS Appointment

### 1. Timing and Arrival
- Arrive **no earlier than 15 minutes** before your scheduled appointment time. Security will not permit early entry into the waiting halls.
- If you arrive more than 30 minutes late, your appointment will be cancelled, and you will need to re-book online.

### 2. Mandatory Items to Carry
- **Original Valid Passport(s):** Current passport and all previous passports.
- **Printed Appointment Confirmation Letter:** Containing your reference barcode and QR code.
- **Payment Receipt / Fee Confirmation:** Proof of statutory and service fee payment.
- **Physical Document Dossier (if not using self-upload):** Organized neatly without staples, clips, or plastic folders.

### 3. Security & Prohibited Items
VFS Global enforces strict consular security protocols:
- **Laptops, tablets, cameras, recording equipment, and USB flash drives** are strictly prohibited inside the centre.
- Mobile phones must be switched to silent mode.
- Luggage and large backpacks are not permitted inside the submission halls.

## Self-Upload vs. Document Scanning Assistance

For missions utilizing digital document upload (such as UKVI and Canada IRCC):
- **Self-Upload (Recommended):** You or your consultant scan and upload clear PDF documents into the correct category slots on the VFS portal prior to attending your appointment. Ensure files are legible and under maximum file size limits (typically 5MB per PDF).
- **Assisted Scanning:** If you choose not to self-upload, you can purchase assisted scanning services at the VFS counter, where staff will scan your physical documents on the day of your appointment.

## Biometric Data Capture Process

During biometric enrolment:
1. **Fingerprint Scanning:** 10-digit digital optical scan without ink or liquids. Ensure your fingertips are clean and free from cuts, temporary henna, or peeling skin.
2. **Digital Facial Photograph:** Taken against a neutral light background. Hats, sunglasses, and head coverings (except for religious or medical purposes where facial features from hairline to chin remain clearly visible) are not permitted.
