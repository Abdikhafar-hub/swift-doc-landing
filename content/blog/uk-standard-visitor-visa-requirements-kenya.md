## Overview of the UK Standard Visitor Visa

The UK Standard Visitor Visa allows Kenyan citizens to visit the United Kingdom for tourism, visiting family and friends, attending business meetings, academic conferences, or undergoing private medical treatment. It is typically granted for 6 months with multiple entries.

All applications from Kenya are submitted online through the UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) portal, followed by biometric enrolment at the VFS Global Visa Application Centre in Nairobi.

## Mandatory Document Checklist for Kenyan Applicants

A complete and well-structured UK visa application requires clear evidence demonstrating your purpose of travel, your financial capacity, and your genuine intention to return to Kenya.

### 1. Proof of Identity & Status
- **Current Passport:** Valid for at least 6 months with minimum 2 blank pages.
- **Previous Passports:** Showing international travel history and compliance.
- **Kenyan National ID Card:** Copy of national identity card.

### 2. Proof of Financial Capability & Employment
- **Bank Statements:** Certified 6-month original bank statements showing steady salary or business cash flow.
- **Employment Letter:** On official company letterhead detailing your job title, date of employment, gross salary, approved annual leave dates, and confirmation of continuing employment upon return.
- **Payslips:** Official payslips for the most recent 3 to 6 months.
- **For Business Owners:** Certificate of Incorporation, CR12 search (less than 90 days old), Business Permit, and company KRA Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC).

### 3. Proof of Accommodation & Travel Plans
- **Detailed Itinerary:** Day-by-day outline of intended activities, travel dates, and internal transit.
- **Accommodation:** Hotel reservations or, if staying with a host, a signed Invitation Letter accompanied by the host's UK passport or BRP copy and proof of accommodation (tenancy agreement or council tax bill).

## Common UK Visa Refusal Reasons in Kenya

UKVI entry clearance officers assess applications under Appendix V of the Immigration Rules. The most common refusal grounds include:

1. **Unexplained Bank Deposits ("Funds Parking"):** Sudden large deposits in personal accounts without documentary proof of origin (such as sale of assets, dividends, or gratuity).
2. **Weak Ties to Kenya:** Insufficient evidence of ongoing employment, property ownership, dependent family, or established business ties.
3. **Discrepancy in Income vs Expenses:** Claiming a monthly income that does not match the actual credits appearing on submitted bank statements.
4. **Vague Travel Purpose:** Submitting generic itineraries without booking confirmations or formal invitations.

## The Application Process in Nairobi

1. **Online Filing:** Complete the online application on the UKVI portal and pay the mandatory statutory visa fee.
2. **Document Upload:** Scan and upload all supporting documents directly to the VFS Global self-upload platform prior to your appointment.
3. **Biometrics Capture:** Attend your scheduled appointment at VFS Global (Nairobi) for fingerprinting and digital photograph capture.
4. **Passport Collection:** Standard processing takes approximately 3 to 6 weeks from biometric enrolment.
