## Overview of the South Africa Visitor Visa (Section 11(1))

Under the South African Immigration Act, foreign nationals from non-exempt categories visiting the Republic of South Africa for holiday, business conferences, medical treatment, or academic events must obtain a Section 11(1) Visitor Visa.

In Kenya, applications are submitted through the **South African Visa Application Centre (VFS Global)** in Nairobi on behalf of the Department of Home Affairs (DHA) and the South African High Commission.

## Document Checklist for Kenyan Applicants

### 1. Mandatory Application Documents
- **DHA Application Form (DHA-84 Form 11):** Completed in black ink and signed by the applicant.
- **Original Passport:** Valid for at least 30 days beyond the intended departure from South Africa, with at least 2 consecutive blank pages.
- **Two Recent Passport Photographs:** White background, meeting DHA specifications.

### 2. Proof of Financial Capability
- **Certified Bank Statements:** Original 3-month bank statements, stamped by the issuing bank on every page. South African DHA requires clear evidence of sufficient funds to cover daily living expenses.
- **Employment Verification:** Original letter from employer stating job designation, salary, date of joining, approved leave duration, and confirmation of return to work.
- **Recent Payslips:** 3 consecutive months of signed or stamped payslips.
- **Business Owners:** Certificate of Incorporation, CR12, company PIN, and 3-month company bank statements.

### 3. Purpose of Visit & Accommodation
- **Tourism:** Verifiable hotel reservation and flight itinerary.
- **Visiting Friends/Family:** Signed invitation letter from the host in South Africa, copy of host's South African ID or valid permanent residence/work visa, and host's recent utility bill or proof of address.
- **Business Visits:** Formal invitation on South African company letterhead detailing the business agenda, signed by an authorized company official, alongside the Kenyan employer's dispatch letter.

## Submission at VFS Global Nairobi

1. Prepare your physical documentation portfolio in the exact sequence mandated by the South African High Commission.
2. Book an appointment slot at VFS Global (South African Visa Centre) in Nairobi.
3. Submit the physical documents, pay the VFS service fee, and submit biometric data (fingerprints and digital photo).
4. Standard processing turnaround is typically **5 to 10 working days**.
