## Start with the filing goal

Company registration begins with clarity on what the business needs to become: a limited company, a business name, a branch registration or another filing under the Business Registration Service. Swift Doc treats this as a preparation exercise before anything is submitted.

The first practical step is to confirm proposed names, director and shareholder details, KRA PIN information and the intended shareholding structure. Small mismatches can slow a file down after submission.

## What Swift Doc checks before submission

- Preferred business or company names
- Identification details for directors, shareholders or proprietors
- KRA PIN certificates where applicable
- Physical, postal and contact details
- Shareholding and appointment details for company files

## After the filing is lodged

Once the file is prepared, Swift Doc follows the filing through the relevant official channel and keeps the client updated on actions required. Statutory fees and processing windows should always be confirmed at the time of filing because requirements can change.
