## Core records to keep accessible

- Business registration certificate or company incorporation documents
- Current director, shareholder or ownership records where applicable
- KRA PIN and tax compliance records
- Relevant licences, permits or professional registrations
- Employer statutory records where the business has staff
- Tender or supplier registration documents where the business bids for work

## Why this matters

Most urgent compliance work begins when a bank, procuring entity, regulator or partner asks for records at short notice. Keeping the file organized reduces panic and makes it easier to identify expired or missing items.
