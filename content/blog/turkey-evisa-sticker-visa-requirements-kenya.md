## Turkey e-Visa vs. Sticker Visa: Which One Do You Need?

Kenyan citizens planning to travel to the Republic of Türkiye for tourism, commercial trade (Istanbul textile, furniture, and machinery sourcing), or medical tourism fall into two distinct regulatory pathways:

### Pathway 1: Turkey Electronic Visa (e-Visa) — Conditional
Kenyan passport holders are eligible for an **instant online e-Visa** ONLY if they hold a **valid supporting visa or residence permit** from one of the following:
- A Schengen Member State
- The United States of America
- The United Kingdom
- Ireland

*Note: The supporting visa must be physically valid at the time of entry into Türkiye. Electronic visas (e-visas) from other nations are not accepted as supporting documents.*

### Pathway 2: Turkey Consular Sticker Visa
Kenyan passport holders who do not possess a valid supporting visa from the USA, UK, Ireland, or Schengen area **must apply for a standard sticker visa** through the Turkish Embassy Consular Section or accredited visa submission centre in Nairobi.

## Requirements for Turkey Sticker Visa in Nairobi

### 1. Primary Documentation
- **Valid Passport:** Valid for at least 6 months with 2 blank pages.
- **Biometric Photos:** Two 5x6cm photographs with white background.
- **Online Pre-Application Form:** Printed and signed application confirmation from the Turkish consular portal.

### 2. Proof of Occupation and Financial Stability
- **Employed:** Formal introduction letter from employer stating title, salary, leave period, and return guarantee; accompanied by 3 months payslips and certified 6 months personal bank statements.
- **Business Owners (Importers/Traders):** Certificate of Incorporation, CR12 search, company PIN, Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC), and 6 months certified company bank statements.
- **Trade Proof:** Invoices, packing lists, or trade agreements with Turkish suppliers if traveling for business.

### 3. Travel and Accommodation Evidence
- **Flight Reservations:** Round-trip flight itinerary (e.g. Kenya Airways / Turkish Airlines).
- **Accommodation:** Confirmed hotel booking in Istanbul or destination city covering the full itinerary.
- **Travel Health Insurance:** Covering medical emergencies in Türkiye.

## Processing Timelines in Nairobi

- **Turkey e-Visa:** Generated within **24 hours** online.
- **Sticker Visa:** Typically requires **7 to 15 working days** following physical appointment submission in Nairobi.
