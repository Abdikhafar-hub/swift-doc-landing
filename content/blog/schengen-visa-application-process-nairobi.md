## What is a Short-Stay Schengen Visa (Type C)?

A Uniform Schengen Visa (Type C) permits Kenyan passport holders to enter and travel freely within the 29 Schengen member countries for a maximum of 90 days within any 180-day period.

Common travel purposes include tourism, visiting friends and family, attending trade fairs or business negotiations, and short training courses.

## The Critical "Main Destination" Rule

Under Article 5 of the EU Visa Code, you cannot randomly pick any European embassy in Nairobi to submit your application. You must apply to:
1. **The country that constitutes your main destination** in terms of the length or purpose of your stay (e.g., spending 6 days in France and 3 days in Italy requires applying at the French Embassy / TLScontact).
2. **If visiting several countries with equal duration**, you must apply at the embassy of the country through whose external border you will first enter the Schengen area.

Applying at the incorrect embassy will result in application rejection or refusal at the consular window.

## Core Document Checklist for Schengen Applications in Nairobi

### 1. Mandatory Application Documents
- **Schengen Visa Application Form:** Fully completed, printed, and signed.
- **Valid Passport:** Valid for at least 3 months beyond intended departure from Schengen, issued within the last 10 years, with at least 2 blank pages.
- **Two Passport Photographs:** 35x45mm, light background, meeting ICAO standards.

### 2. Travel & Accommodation Proof
- **Round-Trip Flight Reservation:** Verifiable flight itinerary (not necessarily a paid ticket until visa is approved).
- **Proof of Accommodation:** Confirmed hotel reservations covering the entire duration across all member states, or an official municipality-certified sponsorship/invitation certificate (e.g., *Attestation d'accueil* for France or *Verpflichtungserklärung* for Germany).
- **Travel Medical Insurance:** Mandatory minimum medical coverage of **EUR 30,000** covering emergency medical expenses, hospitalisation, and repatriation, valid across all Schengen states.

### 3. Financial Ties and Socio-Economic Proof in Kenya
- **Bank Statements:** Certified 3 to 6 months bank statements with clear proof of regular income.
- **Employed Applicants:** Introduction letter from employer stating position, salary, date of employment, approved leave duration, and return confirmation; accompanied by 3 months payslips.
- **Self-Employed Applicants:** KRA PIN certificate, KRA Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC), Certificate of Incorporation/Registration, and active CR12 search (less than 90 days old).
- **Family Ties:** Marriage certificate, birth certificates of dependent children in Kenya.

## Submission Outlets in Nairobi

- **France, Italy, Switzerland, Belgium:** Handled primarily via **TLScontact** (Westlands/Nairobi).
- **Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, Austria, Spain:** Handled via **VFS Global** (Parklands/Westlands/Nairobi).
