## Overview of UAE (Dubai) Tourist Visas for Kenyans

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) offers electronic entry permits for tourism, family visits, transit, and business meetings. For Kenyan passport holders, standard tourist visas are available for stays of **30 days** or **60 days**, issued as single-entry or multiple-entry electronic visas.

All UAE tourist visas are processed digitally through the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) or the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA Dubai).

## Mandatory Document Checklist for Kenyan Applicants

### 1. Primary Identification
- **Scanned Colour Copy of Kenyan Passport:** High-resolution scan of the passport biodata page, valid for at least 6 months from intended date of entry.
- **Passport Photograph:** Digital passport photo with white background (meeting UAE immigration specifications).
- **Previous UAE Visas & Entry Stamps:** If you have visited the UAE previously, scans of prior entry permits and exit stamps.

### 2. Travel & Accommodation Confirmation
- **Return Flight Ticket:** Confirmed return airline booking (originating in Nairobi and departing the UAE before visa expiry).
- **Proof of Accommodation:** Confirmed hotel booking voucher or certified UAE host resident ID and tenancy agreement (*Ejari*).

### 3. Financial and Boarding Requirements
UAE immigration authorities and airlines at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) enforce strict passenger clearance checks:
- Sufficient financial funds for the duration of stay.
- Valid return ticket.
- Hotel accommodation matching the travel duration.

## Visa Categories Available

- **30-Day Single Entry Tourist Visa:** Ideal for short vacations, shopping trips, trade expos (such as GITEX or Arab Health), and quick business visits.
- **60-Day Single Entry Tourist Visa:** Suited for extended family visits, comprehensive market research, or long vacations.
- **Multiple Entry Tourist Visa (30/60 Days):** Useful for business executives traveling frequently between the GCC and Kenya.

## Turnaround Time and Approval

Electronic tourist visa processing for Kenyan applicants typically takes **2 to 4 working days**. Upon approval, the e-Visa is delivered digitally as a PDF entry permit and does not require physical passport stamping prior to travel.
