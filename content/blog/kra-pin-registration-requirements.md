## Information to prepare

KRA PIN registration, recovery or update work commonly requires accurate identity details, contact information and supporting records that match the taxpayer profile.

For companies, the PIN work is usually connected to the business registration record and the directors' or shareholders' details. For individuals, the focus is identity, contact access and the correct taxpayer profile.
