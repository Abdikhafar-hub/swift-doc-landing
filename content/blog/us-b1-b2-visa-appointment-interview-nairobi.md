## Overview of the U.S. B1/B2 Visitor Visa

The U.S. B1/B2 nonimmigrant visa is designed for temporary travel to the United States for business (B1), such as consulting with business associates, attending scientific/educational conventions, or negotiating contracts, and tourism or medical treatment (B2).

For Kenyan applicants, the process requires filing Form DS-160, paying the non-refundable MRV application fee, scheduling an in-person interview at the U.S. Embassy in Nairobi (Gigiri), and appearing before a consular officer.

## The Section 214(b) Presumption of Immigrant Intent

Under U.S. immigration law (INA Section 214(b)), every applicant for a B1/B2 visa is presumed to be an intending immigrant until they establish to the satisfaction of the consular officer that:
1. The purpose of their trip is for a temporary visit.
2. They have a permanent residence in Kenya that they have no intention of abandoning.
3. They possess binding social, economic, and family ties ensuring their return to Kenya.

## Step-by-Step Application Roadmap in Nairobi

### Step 1: Complete the Online Form DS-160
- Accurately provide personal background, employment history, previous international travel, and intended U.S. contact address.
- Upload an ICAO-compliant passport photo (2x2 inches on white background).
- Note down your DS-160 Application ID and print the Confirmation Page with barcode.

### Step 2: Create a Consular Appointment Profile & Pay the MRV Fee
- Register on the official U.S. visa appointment portal for Kenya.
- Generate the payment slip and pay the consular MRV application fee via approved banking channels or online payment.
- Once payment clears, select your interview date at the U.S. Embassy in Nairobi (Gigiri).

### Step 3: Compile Your Interview Portfolio
While consular officers make decisions primarily based on the verbal interview and DS-160 data, you must bring original supporting documentation:
- **DS-160 Confirmation Page** and **Appointment Confirmation Sheet**.
- **Current Passport** (valid for 6+ months) and all previous passports.
- **Employment Proof:** Job contract, introduction letter on corporate letterhead, recent 3 months payslips.
- **Financial Proof:** Certified 6 months personal and/or company bank statements.
- **Business Ownership (if applicable):** Certificate of Incorporation, CR12 search, single business permit, and KRA PIN/TCC.
- **Travel Purpose:** Conference registration, business meeting invitation, or travel itinerary.

## Interview Day Tips at the U.S. Embassy in Gigiri

- **Be Concise and Direct:** Consular interviews typically last 2 to 3 minutes. Answer questions clearly without unnecessary rambling.
- **Match Your DS-160 Exactly:** Any inconsistency between what you say and what was typed in your DS-160 can trigger suspicion.
- **Focus on Kenyan Ties:** Be prepared to articulate your career commitments, family responsibilities, and future projects in Kenya.
