## What a TCC file usually depends on

A Tax Compliance Certificate request can be affected by returns, obligations, ledger balances, penalties or account details on iTax. Swift Doc reviews the client position before advising the next filing step.

## Common blockers

- Unfiled returns
- Old penalties or balances
- Active obligations that no longer match the business activity
- Incorrect account or contact details
- Missing records needed to explain a tax position

Swift Doc does not replace KRA or issue the certificate. The role is to help prepare, review and follow up the client file through the appropriate official channel.
