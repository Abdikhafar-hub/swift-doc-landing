## Common information requested

For business registration work, Swift Doc commonly asks clients for identification, KRA PIN details, passport-size photographs, preferred names and address details. The exact list depends on the filing type.

Requirements should be confirmed before submission because the registry or portal may request additional details depending on ownership, citizenship, company structure or the nature of the proposed business.

## Why preparation matters

The most avoidable delays usually come from mismatched names, incomplete ownership details, unclear document scans or changes made after the filing has started.

Swift Doc reviews the file before lodging it and explains any gaps before the client pays statutory fees or commits to a filing path.
