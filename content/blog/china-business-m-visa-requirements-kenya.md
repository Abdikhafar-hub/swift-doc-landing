## Overview of the Chinese Business (M) Visa

Kenyan entrepreneurs, importers, and corporate executives frequently travel to the People's Republic of China for commercial trade, supplier audits, factory inspections, and exhibitions such as the Canton Fair in Guangzhou or Yiwu International Trade City.

Commercial visits are covered under the **Chinese Business (M) Visa**, which is issued as single, double, or multiple entry depending on prior travel history and company standing.

## Mandatory China M Visa Requirements in Nairobi

All Chinese visa applications in Kenya are processed through the **Chinese Visa Application Service Centre (CVASC)** located in Nairobi (Kilimani / Westlands area).

### 1. The Official Chinese Trade Invitation Letter
The single most critical requirement for an M Visa is an authentic invitation letter from an authorized Chinese partner or exhibition organiser. The letter must contain:
- **Applicant’s Full Details:** Full legal name as in passport, gender, date of birth, and passport number.
- **Visit Details:** Exact purpose of visit, arrival and departure dates, cities to be visited, and relationship between the applicant and the inviting entity.
- **Financial Guarantee:** Clear statement indicating who will bear the travel and accommodation expenses.
- **Inviting Entity Details:** Company official name, Chinese corporate unified social credit code, registered address, telephone number, official red company seal (*Gongzhang*), and signature of legal representative.

For trade exhibitions (e.g. Canton Fair), the official electronic Buyer Invitation Card or VIP Pass from the China Foreign Trade Centre must be presented.

### 2. Kenyan Business Documentation
- **Company Registration:** Certificate of Incorporation or Business Registration Certificate of the Kenyan firm.
- **CR12 Search:** Recent CR12 confirming company directorship.
- **KRA Documentation:** Company KRA PIN and valid Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC).
- **Introduction Letter:** Company letter explaining the trader's role and authorizing the procurement trip to China.
- **Past Trade Proof:** Bills of Lading, customs import declarations, or commercial invoices with Chinese suppliers (strengthens multiple-entry requests).

### 3. Personal & Identity Proof
- **Current Passport:** Valid for at least 6 months with 2 blank visa pages.
- **Previous China Visas:** Clear copies of previous Chinese visas and exit/entry stamps.
- **Bank Statements:** Certified 3-6 months company and personal bank statements.
- **COVA Application Form:** Fully completed China Online Visa Application (COVA) form with barcode confirmation.

## Submission Steps at CVASC Nairobi

1. **Complete the COVA Form:** Fill out the detailed online form with precision.
2. **Schedule CVASC Appointment:** Book an appointment slot on the CVASC portal for Nairobi.
3. **Submit Physical Dossier & Biometrics:** Attend CVASC Nairobi in person with complete physical documents and undergo biometric capture.
4. **Collect Visa:** Standard processing turnaround is typically 4 to 7 working days.
